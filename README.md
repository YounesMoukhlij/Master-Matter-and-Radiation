# Master's Archive: Material Science

## Overview
This repository strictly archives the theoretical coursework, structural analyses, and academic projects completed during my Master's degree in Material Science at the Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Khouribga (FPK), Université Sultan Moulay Slimane (USMS). 

The curriculum documented here established the crystallographic, thermodynamic, and electrochemical framework strictly required for my current doctoral research in the advanced design of high-performance lithium and sodium batteries utilizing machine learning methodologies.

## Academic Profile
* **University:** Université Sultan Moulay Slimane (USMS)
* **Faculty:** Faculté Polydisciplinaire de Khouribga (FPK)
* **Degree:** Master of Science in Material Science

## Core Academic Domains
* **Solid-State Physics & Crystallography:** Structural analysis of crystalline frameworks, coordination polyhedra, and phase stability.
* **Advanced Electrochemistry:** Charge transfer kinetics, intercalation mechanics, and electrochemical thermodynamics.
* **Material Characterization:** Spectroscopic and structural evaluation methodologies.
* **Computational Materials Science:** Foundational theoretical physics (including Density Functional Theory) required for material simulation.

## Repository Structure
* `/crystallography_and_structure/`: Course notes on crystal families and structural frameworks.
* `/electrochemistry_and_thermodynamics/`: Analyses of reaction kinetics and thermodynamic stability.
* `/characterization_techniques/`: Methodological reports on material evaluation.
* `/computational_projects/`: Preliminary scripts and theoretical modeling data.
